# Security Policy

This is the default security policy for every repository in
TheNewsmastFoundation that does not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately so we can fix them before they are
publicly disclosed.

- **Contact:** akp@binarylab.io
- **Do not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion for
  security-sensitive reports.
- Include the affected repository, version or commit, reproduction steps,
  impact, and any suggested fix.

## Response targets

- **Acknowledgement:** within 3 business days.
- **Triage and impact assessment:** within 10 business days.
- **Fix or mitigation:** within 30 days of a confirmed report, faster for
  critical issues.

We will keep you updated and credit you in the release notes unless you ask
us not to.

## Out of scope

- Vulnerabilities in upstream Mastodon (report them to the Mastodon project).
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies (report them upstream; we will
  update once a fix is available).
- Social engineering, physical access, or compromised developer machines.
- Denial of service through unrealistic request volumes.
