The [Newsmast Foundation](https://newsmastfoundation.org/) helps mission-driven organisations create independent, ethical digital spaces through simple, affordable community apps. 

We believe communities deserve online spaces without ads, algorithms or data exploitation, spaces that reflect their values and support genuine connection. 

Our goal is to make the open social web accessible to everyone, providing powerful tools without technical complexity.

## Newsmast Dashboard
A Rails dashboard for enhancing Mastodon servers with community channels, hashtag relays, and content curation tools for the social web.
 - [newsmast-dashboard](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/newsmast-dashboard) (Mastodon)

## Newsmast Plugin
A Ruby gem that extends a Mastodon server with configurable features for posts, accounts, feeds and more.
 - [newsmast-mastodon](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/newsmast-mastodon)

## Newsmast Apps for Change
Bring your people and content together in a safer, calmer, independent space supported by an education charity.
 - [Apps for Change (Android)](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/newsmast-android)
 - [Apps for Change (iOS)](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/newsmast-ios)
