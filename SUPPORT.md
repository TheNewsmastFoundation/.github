# Support

- **Bugs:** open an issue using the **Bug report** form.
- **Feature requests:** open an issue using the **Feature request** form.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open an issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct issues:** follow the reporting steps in
  [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Documentation:** https://github.com/TheNewsmastFoundation/documentation

Issues and pull requests are answered on a best-effort basis, usually within
7 business days.
